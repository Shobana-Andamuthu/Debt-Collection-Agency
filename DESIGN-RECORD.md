**Brand Name:** Aegis Recovery Partners — Ethical & Compliance-First Debt Resolution  
**Industry:** Commercial & Consumer Debt Recovery / Accounts Receivable Management (ARM) / Legal Forwarding  

---

## 1. Business Identity & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:**
  1. *Commercial Enterprise & CFOs:* Corporate controllers, finance directors, and B2B suppliers managing aged trade credit, unpaid invoices, and insolvent vendor accounts.
  2. *Healthcare Systems & Hospital Networks:* Patient financial services directors needing HIPAA-compliant, compassionate medical arrears resolution that protects patient goodwill.
  3. *Fintech & Lending Institutions:* Consumer banking, SaaS subscription, and credit card issuers requiring CFPB Regulation F (7X7) audited recovery velocity.
- **Brand Personality:** Institutional, Sovereign, Highly Compliant, Trustworthy, Authoritative, Transparent, Dignified, Empirical.
- **Key Rules:**
  - Full **Client Recovery Dashboard** included (`dashboard.html`).
  - Dedicated **Login & Register** portal pages (`login.html`, `register.html`).
  - Exactly 7 Main Navbar items (Home with Home 1 & Home 2 dropdown, Services, Compliance & Licensing, Pricing, Recovery Process, About, Contact).
  - Absolutely UNIQUE imagery across all pages (zero image reuse).

## 3. Dedicated Layout Archetypes for Template 3
1. **Financial Certificate / Bond Cards:** Cards feature subtle top gold borders, embossed seal badges, statutory reference tags, and baseline-aligned contingent yield meters.
2. **Forensic Asset Ledger Tables:** Asymmetric split sections showing data tables with UCC lien priorities, bank search metrics, and skip-trace accuracy rates.
3. **50-State Bar & Compliance Matrix:** Grid of jurisdictional compliance checkpoints with automated call frequency limits and time-zone safeguards.
4. **Interactive Recovery Yield Terminal:** Interactive dynamic slider calculating gross vs. net client liquidation value across delinquency buckets (30-60d, 61-120d, 121-365d).
5. **Secure Bank-Grade Debt Intake Vault:** 256-bit SSL encrypted drag-and-drop batch ledger intake portal with 24-hour turnaround scrub guarantee.
